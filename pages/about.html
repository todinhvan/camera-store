We are more than just a retailer; we are a dedicated community of photography enthusiasts committed to providing premium tools for visual storytelling.

Our Story

Starting as a humble, passionate retailer in Vietnam, Camera Store was founded on a simple principle: to bring precision engineering to local visionaries. In our early days, we focused on curating reliable, high-performance gear for a small but growing community of professionals.

Over the years, as the visual landscape evolved, so did we. We expanded our inventory to include the latest in mirrorless technology, cinema cameras, and studio equipment, becoming a premier destination for creators.

Today, our commitment remains unchanged. We provide not just equipment, but expertise, ensuring every photographer and filmmaker has the exact tools they need to execute their vision flawlessly.
